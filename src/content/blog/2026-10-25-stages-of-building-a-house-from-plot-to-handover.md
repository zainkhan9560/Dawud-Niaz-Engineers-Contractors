Building a house is easier to manage when you understand the order of work. Every project is different, but most follow the same broad stages. Knowing them helps you ask the right questions and spot problems early.

## 1. Planning and drawings

Start with your requirements and budget. Then prepare [architectural](/services/architectural-drawings-lahore) and [structural drawings](/services/structural-drawings-lahore), and a cost estimate. Check the current approval requirements with the relevant authority.

## 2. Site preparation

The site is cleared and marked out according to the drawings. Access for materials and workers is arranged, and storage for cement, steel and other materials is set up.

## 3. Excavation and foundations

Trenches are dug, reinforcement is placed and concrete is poured for the foundations. This stage is hidden once it is covered, so it is the time to check carefully that the work matches the structural drawings.

## 4. Structure

Columns, beams and slabs go up floor by floor. Formwork, reinforcement and concrete are all checked at each stage. Curing, which means keeping new concrete damp for the period your engineer specifies, is an important part of getting strong concrete.

## 5. Brickwork

Walls are built to the plan, leaving openings for doors and windows. Check alignment, thickness and openings against the drawings before plastering begins.

## 6. Services

Electrical conduits, plumbing lines and drainage are laid in walls and floors before plastering and flooring. Changing these later is messy and costly, so confirm positions in advance.

## 7. Plaster and finishing

Plaster, flooring, tiles, paint, doors, windows and fixtures complete the building. Finishing choices have a large effect on the final cost, so decide them early.

## 8. Final checks and handover

Walk through the building with your contractor and make a list of any items that need correcting. Keep a copy of the drawings and any records of the work.

## Tips for the whole project

- Link payments to completed stages rather than paying large amounts upfront.
- Put the agreed scope and specifications in writing.
- Make sure someone qualified checks the work regularly.
- Ask before changing the plan during construction, because changes affect cost and time.

If you are comparing builders, ask each one for a written scope, a bill of quantities and a clear payment schedule so the quotations can be compared fairly.

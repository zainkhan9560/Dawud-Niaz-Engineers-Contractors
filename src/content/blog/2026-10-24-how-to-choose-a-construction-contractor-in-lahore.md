Choosing the contractor is one of the biggest decisions in any building project. A good choice saves money and stress. A poor one can cost you months. Here are ten practical questions to ask before you sign anything.

## 1. Can I see completed projects like mine?

Ask for photos and, if possible, visit a finished or ongoing site. A contractor who builds banks and plazas may not be the right fit for a ten-marla house, and the reverse is also true. Look for work similar in size and type to yours.

## 2. Who will supervise my site every day?

Many problems come from poor supervision rather than poor materials. Ask who will be on site, how often, and whether an engineer reviews the work. [House construction](/services/house-construction-lahore) goes better when someone qualified checks each stage.

## 3. Will you give me a written quotation with the scope?

A price without a scope is only a number. Ask for a written quotation that lists what is included, such as excavation, structure, brickwork, plaster, electrical, plumbing and finishing, and what is not included. This is the only way to compare two contractors fairly.

## 4. What is the difference between grey structure and complete construction?

Grey structure usually means the structure, walls and roof before finishing works. Complete construction adds finishing. Contracts differ, so ask for the exact items in writing. You can get a rough idea of the budget with our [cost calculator](/cost-calculator).

## 5. How will payments be linked to progress?

Avoid paying large amounts upfront. Payments should follow completed stages, such as foundation, columns and slab, brickwork, plastering and finishing. A clear payment schedule protects both sides.

## 6. Who provides the materials, and to what specification?

Decide early whether the contractor or you will supply materials. Whichever you choose, write down the brands or specifications for cement, steel, bricks, tiles and fixtures so there are no arguments later.

## 7. Do you work from proper drawings?

Construction without complete [architectural](/services/architectural-drawings-lahore) and [structural drawings](/services/structural-drawings-lahore) leads to changes on site, and changes cost money. Ask whether the contractor prepares drawings or builds from yours, and make sure the two sets match.

## 8. What is the timeline, and what happens if it slips?

Ask for a realistic schedule with milestones. Delays do happen, so agree in advance how they will be communicated and handled.

## 9. How do you handle changes during construction?

You may change your mind about a room or finish. Ask how variations are priced and approved, and insist that changes are written down before the work is done.

## 10. Can I talk to previous clients?

References tell you what a brochure cannot: whether the contractor kept the schedule, stayed close to the budget and fixed problems quickly.

## Warning signs

- A price that is far below every other quotation, with no clear scope
- Pressure to pay a large advance
- No written agreement
- Unwillingness to show completed work or give references
- No clear answer on who supervises the site
